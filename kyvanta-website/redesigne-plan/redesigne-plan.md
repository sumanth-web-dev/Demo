# 1. FIRST PRINCIPLE: DO NOT BLINDLY ACCEPT THE CURRENT STRATEGY

Before changing anything, perform a critical audit.

Do not assume the current positioning, services, terminology, target customers, claims, navigation, CTAs, business model, or content are correct.

For every important existing element ask:

1. Why does this exist?
2. Who is it for?
3. What business problem does it solve?
4. Does it differentiate Kyvanta?
5. Does it help generate qualified leads?
6. Is it credible?
7. Can it be explained more clearly?
8. Is there evidence supporting the claim?
9. Does it make commercial sense?
10. Should it be kept, changed, moved, combined, or removed?

If an idea is weak, say so.

Do NOT preserve something merely because it already exists.
